# UI Healer

## Step 1

Step 1 is to take a screenshot of each screen in question using the Palywright MCP

## Step 2

Step 2, refernce the directory /guide/ and find the file style_guide.html, main_landing.html and admin_dashboard.html 

Based on these files you are to grade the output of Step 1 objectively against that standard, colors etc and give your response on a scale of 1 to 10. 

## Step 3

For any screens or components that have a score of less than 8 out of 10, you must make changes (without impacting app functionality - only UI related changes), and then repeat from step 1
